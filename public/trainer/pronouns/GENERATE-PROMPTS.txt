--------------------------------------

A pencil-sketch character sheet on a piece of warm cream sketchbook paper with slightly torn edges — the paper itself is part of the artwork.
Character: a spirited cartoon girl in the spirit of 90s adventure-cartoon heroines — big expressive emerald eyes, golden-blonde hair in a loose high ponytail, a lilac mechanic jumpsuit with rolled-up sleeves, a wrench peeking from her pocket. Confident, clever, playful posture — a subtle flirtatious wink, tasteful pin-up charm, never vulgar.
Style: loose expressive graphite pencil strokes, visible hatching, soft smudged shading, one gentle watercolor accent on hair and jumpsuit. Warm light from the left.
Pose: relaxed three-quarter view, hands on hips, cheerful smirk. Square 1:1 composition, character centered, generous paper margin around her.
